Privacy Policy

BLEGraphApp

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

BLEGraphApp is an application designed to receive, display, and locally store measurement data from a compatible external Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) device.

The application is intended for use with designated measurement equipment. It cannot perform its primary functionality without a compatible Bluetooth device.

2. Data Collection

BLEGraphApp does not collect or transmit user information or measurement data to the developer or external servers.

The application does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics services.

3. Local Data Storage

Measurement data received through Bluetooth is stored locally on the user's iPad.

Measurement data is not automatically uploaded to cloud services, external databases, or developer-operated servers.

4. Bluetooth Communication

Bluetooth access is required to establish a connection with a compatible external BLE device and receive measurement data.

Bluetooth communication is used solely for the application's measurement functionality.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not access, sell, or share measurement data stored within the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Measurement data remains stored locally on the iPad until it is deleted through the application's available functions or the application and its associated local data are removed from the device.

7. Data Security

The application relies on the security mechanisms provided by iPadOS for local application storage and Bluetooth access.

Users and device administrators are responsible for maintaining appropriate device access controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application developer through the official application support channel.
