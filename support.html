BLEGraphApp – Support

Technical support and user information

1. About BLEGraphApp

BLEGraphApp is an iPad application designed to communicate with a compatible external Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) measurement device.

The application receives and displays measurement data in real time, allows users to configure measurement parameters, and supports local storage of measurement data.

2. System Requirements

BLEGraphApp requires:

  • A compatible Apple iPad running iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Bluetooth functionality enabled.
  • A compatible external BLE measurement device.
Important: BLEGraphApp requires a compatible external Bluetooth measurement device to perform its primary functions. The application cannot independently acquire measurement data.

3. Connecting the Bluetooth Device

  1. Make sure the external measurement device is powered on.
  2. Enable Bluetooth on the iPad.
  3. Open BLEGraphApp.
  4. Allow Bluetooth access if prompted.
  5. Start the measurement function within the application.

4. Troubleshooting

No Bluetooth Connection

  • Check that the external device is powered on and within Bluetooth range.
  • Verify that Bluetooth is enabled on the iPad.
  • Check Bluetooth permissions for BLEGraphApp in iPadOS Settings.
  • Restart the app and try again.

No Measurement Data

  • Verify that the compatible device is connected and operating correctly.
  • Check that the measurement function has been started.
  • Restart the application if necessary.

5. Data Storage and Privacy

Measurement data is stored locally on the iPad. The application does not transmit measurement data to developer-operated servers.

For further information, please read our Privacy Policy.
